This can be as short as a few lines. Write about what has been occupying your
time, something you noticed, or a moment you would like to remember.

## Lately

Add a life update, a photograph, a book, a walk, or a small thing that made the
week feel different. The shape of the page can follow the shape of the thought.

## A small discovery

Leave a link or a note for your future self. These headings are only starting
points; remove them if the entry reads better without them.
