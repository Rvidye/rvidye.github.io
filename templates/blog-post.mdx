Begin wherever the story begins. There is no need for an abstract or a formal
introduction; a clear observation is enough.

## Following the thought

Give the idea room to develop. Use a heading when the subject changes, and leave
it out when a few connected paragraphs tell the story better.

> A short quotation or a thought to return to can live here.

End when you have said what you came to say. Links, a photograph, or a small
aside are welcome if they belong to the story.
