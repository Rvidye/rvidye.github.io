Open with the question in plain language. Explain why it is interesting before
describing the machinery used to study it.

## The question

Give the reader the context needed to understand the problem. Be specific about
the scope and what this work tries to find out.

## The approach

Explain the central idea and your contribution. A figure or a short video can
make the method easier to follow.

## What we know so far

Describe the evidence, its limits, and the current state of the work. Keep
measured results distinct from expectations.

## People and context

Credit collaborators and link to relevant work. Add confirmed publications in
frontmatter when they are ready to be shared.
