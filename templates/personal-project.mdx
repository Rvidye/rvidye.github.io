Start with what drew you to the idea. A small story, an image, or a working demo
can say more here than a list of features.

## The idea

What did you want to make? What made it interesting to you? Keep enough context
for someone arriving at this page for the first time.

## Making it

Describe a decision, a wrong turn, or something you learned while building.
Add images or a video where they help tell the story.

## Where it stands

Share what works, what is unfinished, and what you might try next. Omit any
section that does not fit the project.
