My journey into computer graphics started with **AstromediComp's Real-Time Rendering (RTR) batch**, where I was introduced to OpenGL and, more importantly, computer graphics in general. I later became a group leader for the next RTR batch while simultaneously working as a **Visualization Software Engineer at Dassault Systèmes** in Pune, India.

I wanted to explore the research side of computer graphics and build a stronger foundation in the field, which led me to pursue my **MS in Computing (Graphics & Visualization)** at the **University of Utah**. I'm currently doing research with [Dr. Cem Yuksel](https://www.cemyuksel.com/) in the [Utah Graphics Lab](https://graphics.cs.utah.edu/) and working with the [Hardware Ray Tracing group](https://hwrt.cs.utah.edu/).

My interests span physically based rendering, geometry processing, GPU architecture, and performance optimization. I particularly enjoy building things from scratch, experimenting with new ideas, and figuring out why something that *should* be faster somehow isn't.

I'm also a [**Teaching Assistant for CS 4600: Introduction to Computer Graphics**](https://graphics.cs.utah.edu/courses/cs4600/fall2026/).
