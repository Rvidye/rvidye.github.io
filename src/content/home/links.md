## Useful things

A few references behind the projects here:

- [Physically Based Rendering](https://pbr-book.org/): the book behind much of FoxRT.
- [Ray Tracing in One Weekend](https://raytracing.github.io/), where my earlier ray-tracing experiments started.
- [Inigo Quilez's SDF shape reference](https://iquilezles.org/articles/distfunctions/): signed distance fields, as used in MayaSabha.
