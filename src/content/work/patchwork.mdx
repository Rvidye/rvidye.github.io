Patchwork explores a different approach to polygonal modeling, where you work with high-level **meta-patches, meta-edges, and meta-vertices** instead of directly manipulating the underlying mesh.

<Video src="/media/patchwork/standalone-demo.mp4" title="Modeling with meta-patches in the standalone Patchwork application" poster={standalonePoster} width={1920} height={1008} loop>
  The original standalone application: extruding, carving, and rotating meta-vertices and meta-faces.
</Video>

One of our more interesting features is the ability to convert any selected meta-patch into a **generalized Bézier patch**, so you can switch between polygonal and parametric modeling within the same model. Similarly, meta-edges can be represented and manipulated as Bézier curves. This gives much more flexibility when modeling smooth surfaces and controlling their boundaries.

<Video src="/media/patchwork/bezier-teapot.mp4" title="Editing the Utah teapot through its Bézier meta-patches" poster={bezierPoster} width={1024} height={1024} loop>
  The teapot as generalized Bézier patches, reshaped through their control points.
</Video>

## My contribution

I first worked on the original standalone application, implementing interactive modeling tools including **Mirror Editor, Carve, and Extrude**. We presented the framework at **SIGGRAPH 2026**, where I also demonstrated the modeling workflow live.

For my master's project, I'm now reimplementing the framework as a **Blender add-on**, with a C++ geometry-processing backend and a Python UI and interactions. This involves building a custom half-edge mesh representation, implementing the geometry-processing and weighting algorithms, and integrating the modeling tools into Blender.

<Video src="/media/patchwork/blender-add-on.mp4" title="The Patchwork Blender add-on, turning a cube into a twisted shape" poster={blenderPoster} width={1920} height={1146} loop>
  Work in progress: the Blender add-on, from a cube to a twisted shape.
</Video>

The goal is to make these modeling techniques accessible within Blender while continuing to experiment with new modeling interactions.
